01 / Why we study

Social difficulty is not a defect. It is one part of the broad spectrum of human diversity.

Our mission is to use careful, compassionate science to understand where connection becomes difficult—and to help create conditions in which more people can participate, relate, and live freely as themselves.

✦

We study people, not just data points.

Rigor and humanity belong in the same room.

How we work

Many signals.
One human story.

No single measure can explain a social life. We combine perspectives and methods to trace mechanisms across experience, behavior, body, brain, and context.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Qualitative inquiry and lived experience

  2. 02

    Measure

    Surveys, psychometrics, and longitudinal designs

  3. 03

    Observe

    Behavioral experiments and eye tracking

  4. 04

    Map

    Psychophysiology and neuroimaging

  5. 05

    Model

    AI-assisted, interpretable analysis of social interaction

  6. 06

    Translate

    Precise, practical, evidence-based interventions
